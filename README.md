About Myself
I am an M.S. graduate and PhD student in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science at Florida Atlantic University (FAU), as well as an inventor, software engineer, author, DAV, and entrepreneur. As a Tampa-based software developer, I love architecting, coding, and creating web, native, and IoT embedded systems applications. I have contributed to notable engineering projects, research initiatives, and internships, successfully delivering greenfield AI software products from conception to market.

My expertise spans Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), Agentic AI, Computer Vision, and Edge AI, including model architecture, training, fine-tuning, quantization, neural network optimization, and embedded C++ inference engineering. I enjoy bridging AI research with practical software solutions, integrating cloud-based intelligence with real-time systems and resource-constrained hardware.

I am passionate about AI, data science, mathematics, programming, and physics, particularly artificial intelligence, quantum computing, control theory, and convex optimization algorithms (the fun stuff). I strive to use my engineering skills, research, and creativity to solve complex business challenges and problems related to my passions.

The problems I'm most passionate about include artificial intelligence applications in deep space, progress for humanity, oceanography, sharks, and state security.

In addition to reading, playing guitar, surfing, and traveling, I enjoy creating content and sharing what I've learned with others. I genuinely enjoy helping people and simplifying complex, abstract concepts to make them more understandable.
If we can put our minds together, please don't hesitate to contact me. I'm always open to connecting and collaborating with like-minded hackers, engineers, researchers, scientists, and entrepreneurs.
